## 2. **The problem**

> Every developer has 50 repos and no idea what story they tell.
>
> The startup you should be building isn't on Twitter or in a YC essay.
>
> It's already in your git history. You just can't read it from the inside.

Visual: a wall of repo names, slightly faded. One highlighted in orange.

---

## 3. **The solution**

> Drop a GitHub username. We read every repo, language signal, and commit cadence.
> Out comes a magazine-style report:
>
> – your archetype (one of ten)
> – your signature pattern (the through-line you didn't see)
> – your *next startup* (a real idea, not a category)
>
> Built for builders who already shipped — and the ones about to.

Visual: side-by-side screenshot of input → report.

## 6. **Roadmap — what we're shipping during HACKHAZARDS**

> **Now (v1, shipped May 12):** Public report generator for any GitHub. Editorial UI, OG share cards, in-memory cache.
>
> **Next 6 weeks (v2):**
> – **Founder Match** — graph-based pairing. Your GitHub vs. the global archive: "structurally compatible co-founders" *(Neo4j track candidate).*

> – **Team Scan** — point at an org, get a composite report on its builders.
